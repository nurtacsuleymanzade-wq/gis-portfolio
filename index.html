About

GIS Specialist · PhD researcher

I am a GIS Specialist and PhD student in tourism and spatial analysis, producing cartography and spatial products for destination planning, regional research, and academic publication — with a focus on Azerbaijan and the South Caucasus.

Work spans advanced QGIS and ArcGIS cartography, buffering and overlay analysis, density mapping, interactive WebGIS, and multi-platform production (MapInfo, PostGIS, GeoServer, Mapbox). Layers come from real sources (OSM / Geofabrik, geoBoundaries, SRTM, curated inventories), styled for print or web.

Deliverables include clear packages (PNG / PDF / GeoPackage where needed) and optional PyQGIS / Python automation so updates stay repeatable.
