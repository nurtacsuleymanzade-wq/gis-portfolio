Photorealistic 3D terrain — Shahdag / Quba, Azerbaijan

Blender Cycles render (GPU, 256 samples, denoised) of a real SRTM elevation model draped with Sentinel-2 cloudless imagery. 3840 × 2160 px.

Full 4K JPG (3840×2160) Other samples

Technique

  • Elevation: SRTM 1-arc-second tiles mosaicked and reprojected to UTM 39N (EPSG:32639) at 30 m with GDAL; ~60 × 54 km window covering Shahdag, Bazarduzu, Tufandag, Quba and Qusar. DEM range 257–4,457 m.
  • Texture: EOX Sentinel-2 cloudless mosaic requested through WMS and warped by GDAL onto exactly the same UTM grid (≈15 m texel), so imagery and relief line up pixel-for-pixel.
  • 3D: a 1,007 × 894 vertex mesh built directly from the height array with Blender's Python API (NumPy), 1.6× vertical exaggeration, physically based Nishita sky for sun and atmosphere.
  • Render: Cycles on an NVIDIA L4 (CUDA), 256 samples + OpenImageDenoise, Filmic colour management. 4K frame in about 90 seconds.
Note: the vertical scale is exaggerated 1.6× for legibility. This is a visualisation, not a measurement product. Imagery is the 2016 cloud-free composite, so seasonal snow cover is not shown.

Who benefits / value

Who benefits. Tourism & resorts (Shahdag ski area brochures, trail posters), mining & exploration (concession context visuals for investor decks), academia (study-area figures for theses and journal papers), and municipalities / regional planners communicating terrain to non-specialists. Value: a print-ready 4K image built from real elevation and imagery, reproducible for any area on Earth within a day.
