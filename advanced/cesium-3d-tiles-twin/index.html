Cesium 3D Tiles digital twin from LiDAR

A classified LiDAR point cloud converted to the OGC 3D Tiles standard (.pnts, tiled), streamed in CesiumJS on a global 3D globe. This is the same pipeline used for city-scale digital twins, scaled down to one neighbourhood.

2.62 Mpoints (0.3 m voxel)
17tiles (1 root + 16 children)
OGC 3D Tiles 1.0format
4classes: ground, low veg, trees, buildings

Interactive viewer

Colours: brown = ground, green = vegetation, orange = buildings. Drag to rotate, right-drag to zoom, Ctrl+drag to tilt.

Method

  1. Source: USGS 3DEP LiDAR (CO_DRCOG 2020), cleaned and classified with PDAL (see the LiDAR sample).
  2. Points thinned to a 0.3 m voxel grid and coloured by ASPRS class.
  3. Reprojected from UTM 13N to ECEF (EPSG:4978) with pyproj, written as tiled .pnts files with RTC centres and a tileset.json (ADD refinement, region bounding volumes).
  4. Streamed with CesiumJS 1.120 over OpenStreetMap imagery, with no Cesium ion token and no paid services.

Limitations of this sample

  • Heights are normalised to the local ground (the globe has no terrain here), so the cloud sits on the ellipsoid and is not on NAVD88 or ellipsoidal heights.
  • Simple 2-level tiling. Production twins use octree LOD with geometric error tuned per level (py3dtiles / Cesium ion / 3d-tiles-tools).
  • No building meshes. A LoD2 version would add the roof planes from the roof sample as extruded solids.
