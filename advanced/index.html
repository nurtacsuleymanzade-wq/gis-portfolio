3D & Advanced samples

Real working outputs built from open data with free software: GPU rendering, large-data web mapping, LiDAR and photogrammetry.

Photorealistic 3D terrain (4K)
Blender Cycles GPU render of real SRTM + Sentinel-2, Shahdag / Quba.
165k-point WebGL map
MapLibre GL, real OSM POIs for the Caucasus, 3D terrain toggle.
LiDAR: denoise, classify, DTM/DSM
PDAL pipeline on USGS 3DEP, ground / building / vegetation, Three.js viewer.
Drone orthomosaic → COG
OpenDroneMap photogrammetry, Cloud-Optimized GeoTIFF, Leaflet tiles.
Baku 3D city model
OSM buildings extruded over Sentinel-2, MapLibre 3D + Cycles render.

Unreal Engine 5 work → UE 5.8 interior renders and Cesium pipeline tests.

GPU-heavy analysis samples

GPU 2D flood simulation (Kura–Aras)
72 h shallow-water model on an L4 GPU, 1.7 M cells, plus a Cycles render and time-lapse.
LiDAR roof-plane reconstruction
609 roof facets with pitch, aspect and area from USGS 3DEP LiDAR, 3D viewer and roof report.
GPU solar & shadow analysis
558 sun positions ray-traced over a 0.5 m DSM, annual kWh/m² per roof facet.
Mineral alteration mapping, Gedabek
Sentinel-2 SWIR ratios and Crosta PCA for clay and iron-oxide alteration around the Cu–Au mine.
Cesium 3D Tiles digital twin
2.6 M classified LiDAR points as OGC 3D Tiles, streamed in CesiumJS.

All outputs on this page were produced from open data with free software (Blender, PDAL, GDAL, OpenDroneMap, MapLibre GL, Three.js). Compute: a cloud Linux workstation with NVIDIA L4 GPUs.
