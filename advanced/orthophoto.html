Drone orthophoto mosaic — OpenDroneMap → COG → web

77 real drone photos from the public OpenDroneMap Aukerman sample dataset (Ohio, USA) processed into a georeferenced orthomosaic with ODM, then published as a Cloud-Optimized GeoTIFF and web tiles.

Download COG (10 cm, JPEG, 3 MB) Preview JPG

Technique

  • Photogrammetry: OpenDroneMap (Docker) on a 16-core Linux server: OpenSfM feature matching and structure-from-motion, 2.5D mesh, ortho-rectification (--fast-orthophoto, 5 cm/px). Output: 7,268 × 5,421 px, UTM 17N (EPSG:32617).
  • COG: GDAL resample to 10 cm, JPEG-compressed Cloud-Optimized GeoTIFF with internal overviews and alpha mask. It streams in QGIS, ArcGIS Pro or any COG-aware web client.
  • Web: gdal2tiles XYZ tiles (zoom 15–20) shown in Leaflet over OpenStreetMap.
This is the fast-orthophoto mode (no full 3D textured model, no ground control points), so absolute accuracy relies on the camera GPS (typically a few metres). The geometry inside the mosaic is consistent.

Who benefits / value

Who benefits. Construction & architecture firms (up-to-date site basemaps), agriculture (field inspection), mining (pit and stockpile mapping), municipalities (cadastre and permit checks), drone operators who need processing outsourced. Value: drone photos turned into a georeferenced, cloud-optimized orthomosaic that streams in any GIS or web map, without paid photogrammetry licences.
