Baku 3D city model — Icherisheher & Sabail

1,736 OpenStreetMap building footprints extruded to height over Sentinel-2 imagery. It comes as an interactive MapLibre 3D scene and a Blender Cycles render.

Open interactive 3D map Render 2560×1440

Technique

  • Footprints from the Geofabrik Azerbaijan OSM extract (GDAL OSM driver), clipped to 49.815–49.865 E, 40.352–40.378 N.
  • Height rules: OSM height if present (19 buildings), else building:levels × 3.2 m (848), else a 9 m placeholder (869). Colour-coded in the web map so the source of every height is transparent.
  • Web: MapLibre GL fill-extrusion on AWS terrain tiles + EOX Sentinel-2 cloudless WMTS (no API token).
  • Render: footprints reprojected to UTM 39N and extruded with Blender bmesh, on a Sentinel-2 ground plane (flat, since the relief here is minimal), then rendered with Cycles on GPU.
An indicative LOD1 massing model, not a surveyed model. Roof shapes are flat and about half the heights are placeholders.

Who benefits / value

Who benefits. Architects & urban designers (massing context around a plot), real-estate developers (marketing and view studies), municipalities & heritage bodies (Icherisheher UNESCO buffer-zone communication), tourism (interactive old-city maps), academia (urban morphology studies). Value: a quick 3D context model from open data, upgradeable to surveyed heights when a client supplies them.
