Method

  1. Data: USGS 3DEP LiDAR, CO_DRCOG 2020 (EPT on AWS), about 380 × 380 m pulled by bounds.
  2. PDAL: statistical + ELM noise removal, SMRF ground, height above ground, building class from planarity.
  3. Building points clustered with DBSCAN (1.2 m); iterative RANSAC fits up to 8 planes per building, refined with SVD.
  4. For each plane: pitch from the normal, aspect (azimuth), and sloped area = 0.5 m occupied cells / cos(pitch).
  5. Solar value per plane is taken from the GPU sun/shadow model (see the solar sample).

What I deliver on a client project

  • Per-building roof report: facets, pitch, aspect, sloped area, ridge/eave heights (CSV / PDF / GeoJSON)
  • LoD2 building models (CityGML / OBJ / IFC) from LiDAR or drone DSM
  • Change detection between flights (new extensions, damaged roofs)
  • Processing of your own LAS/LAZ or photogrammetry point clouds

Limitations of this sample

  • Up to 8 planes per building; complex roofs (dormers, chimneys) are merged into the main facets.
  • Footprint shown is the convex hull of roof points, not a surveyed outline.
  • Point density is about 20 pts/m², so small facets under about 5 m² may be missed. Accuracy is not checked against ground survey.
