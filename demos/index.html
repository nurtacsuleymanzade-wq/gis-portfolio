GIS / LOCATION INTELLIGENCE

Spatial Site Selector

Rank fictional candidate sites using transparent weighted multi-criteria scoring and Haversine distance to a configurable service hub.

Weights normalize automatically
All site coordinates and attributes are synthetic examples. Coordinates use a fictional local cluster; distances are calculated geodesically using the Haversine formula. This is a demonstration, not a planning recommendation.

Scoring formula

Score = w₁ × Demand + w₂ × Proximity + w₃ × (100 − Risk)
Proximity = max(0, 100 − 12 × distance_km)
Weights are normalized to sum to 1.
