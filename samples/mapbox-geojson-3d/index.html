How it is built

MapLibre GL JS 4.7 (open-source fork of Mapbox GL JS) with the same style-spec, GeoJSON sources, fill-extrusion and terrain APIs a Mapbox GL job would use. A client Mapbox token would swap in Mapbox streets or satellite-streets without changing the GeoJSON or interaction code. Terrain is AWS Terrain Tiles (Terrarium / SRTM). Building heights use OSM height or building:levels × 3.2 m; three untagged footprints use a 9 m default.

Honest limits

OSM at Shahdag is good but not complete: 12 lifts and 30 downhill segments are mapped, against about 20 lifts and 37 slopes on the official resort site, and no black/advanced grades are tagged yet. Gaya Residences is listed by the resort but is not named in this extract, so it is not added. Names follow OSM, including local spellings (Skalini, Aspen Qril). This is an indicative 3D resort map, not a surveyed as-built or an official trail map. No AI-generated geography.
