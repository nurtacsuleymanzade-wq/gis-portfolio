Browser 360° tour — demo home

Tap the white dots or the room buttons to move between viewpoints. Works on phone and desktop from a single link, nothing to install.

Built for this sample as a stand-in for a supplied Revit model: modelled, furnished and landscaped in Blender, five 2:1 equirectangular panoramas rendered in Cycles (AgX colour), then stitched into a linked tour with Pannellum. The same pipeline takes a Revit export (FBX/IFC) with your materials and finishes.
