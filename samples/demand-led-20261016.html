01 / GIS analysis & vector handoff

A 50 m setback. Twelve fixed anchors. Editable artwork.

A spatial brief often needs both defensible geometry and files a designer can keep editing. This synthetic example combines a metric stream buffer and vegetation overlay with an SVG master whose location IDs and geographic anchors remain fixed when labels move.

EPSG:32638 → WGS8412 addressable SVG groupsRepeatable Python source

Delivered

GeoJSON layers, stable-ID CSV, editable SVG, reproducible code, area reconciliation and rebuild checksums. The 700,000 m² invented site balances to excluded and remaining areas without unexplained loss.

Scope

Original synthetic geometry. This is a workflow demonstration, not a surveyed site, land-clearance permission, a client’s 67-city dataset, or a PostGIS performance benchmark.

02 / Blender texture delivery

Studio lighting, carried in one diffuse atlas.

An original ceramic loop and original HDR studio environment demonstrate a real Blender bake. Diffuse color plus direct and indirect light are baked into a 1024 × 1024 texture, with portable Blender files that have their image assets packed.

This is a diffuse-light delivery. View-dependent specular reflections are outside its scope. Source model, HDR environment, atlas, comparison renders and rebuild script are included.

03 / AI production & batch automation

One reviewed master. Three approved variants.

An original fictional ceramic fragrance product was generated and animated with Nova, reviewed for object consistency, then passed through a Python/FFmpeg template. Editable JSON briefs control the headline, footer, audio tone and approval state.

What the working batch proves

Three five-second MP4 exports at 720 × 720 with audio, per-output metadata and hashes. The unapproved draft is skipped. Source clip, JSON brief, script and measured logs are included.

Scope

These are three copy/template variants of one AI clip. This small batch does not claim hundreds of daily videos, social account publishing, a deployed n8n service, voiceover or fidelity to a real client product.
