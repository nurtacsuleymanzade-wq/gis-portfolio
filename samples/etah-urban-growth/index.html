Etah (Uttar Pradesh): where new construction happened, 2018-19 → 2025-26

A satellite change analysis of Etah town and its surroundings (window of about 12 × 12 km). It uses Sentinel-2 L2A imagery (ESA Copernicus, via Microsoft Planetary Computer) and OSM major roads.

Method

  1. Eight cloud-free scenes per year spread across the seasons (T0: 2018-10-14, 2018-10-29, 2018-11-18, 2018-12-08, 2019-03-08, 2019-04-22, 2019-05-07, 2019-07-01; T1: 2025-10-12, 2025-11-06, 2025-11-26, 2026-01-10, 2026-02-24, 2026-04-10, 2026-05-10, 2026-06-14), all from the same tile (44RKR).
  2. Built-up = median NDBI > −0.02 and annual maximum NDVI < 0.35. Using the annual NDVI maximum stops fields that are bare after harvest from being counted as buildings. A 3×3 majority filter is applied.
  3. Change = built-up at T1 and not at T0. Results are summarised by 8 compass sectors and by 300 m road corridors.
Limitations: 10 m pixels measure built-up surface, not house counts, and new roads, brick kilns and hard-standing are included (the red lines are new road and rail corridors). Some recorded change is spectral noise around village edges. Accuracy has not yet been checked against high-resolution imagery. Nagar/ward names are not in OSM here, so the per-nagar breakdown needs ward boundaries.

What the full delivery includes

A 10-year series (Landsat 2015 plus Sentinel-2) with accuracy assessment on Google Earth/Planet reference points, building-footprint counts (Google Open Buildings or Microsoft footprints, 2016 vs 2023 or later) to quantify houses, not just area, a ward/nagar boundary join with growth tables for established and emerging nagars, a direction and road-corridor ranking, maps, and a short written report.
